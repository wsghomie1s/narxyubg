Original 3D football. Fictional teams, built from scratch.

How to Play

Offense: pick a play. Run plays hand off to your back. On passes, steer the QB, then throw with keys 1-5.

Moving: WASD / arrows, gamepad left stick. Hold Shift to sprint. Space jukes.

Defense: pick Man, Zone or Blitz. Control the highlighted defender.

Career: start at the Pro Combine. Your drill scores decide your draft round. Impress on your 1-year rookie deal to earn a bigger contract.
